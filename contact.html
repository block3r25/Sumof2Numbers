Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions from library users.

How can I borrow a book?

Log in to your library account, search for the book you need, and check its availability. You can then visit the library to complete the borrowing process.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the password recovery option on the login page or contact the library administrator for assistance.

How can I check my borrowed books?

After logging into your account, open your dashboard to view your currently borrowed books and their due dates.

Can I suggest a new book?

Yes. Send us a message through the contact form with the title and author of the book you would like the library to consider.
